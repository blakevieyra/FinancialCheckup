Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Financial Checkup (“the App”) is operated by OPERON E2I LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (stored securely; passwords are hashed).
  • Financial profile data you enter: income, expenses, debts, savings, investments, insurance, and retirement details used to calculate your score and recommendations.
  • Usage data: basic logs needed to operate the service (e.g., sign-in times, errors).
  • Optional notifications: if you enable email or SMS digests, we store your contact preferences.

We do not connect to your bank accounts or use Plaid unless you explicitly authorize a future integration.

How we use information

  • Provide scoring, reports, and personalized guidance inside the App.
  • Save your profile and history so you can track progress over time.
  • Send account-related emails (verification, password reset, optional digests).
  • Process subscriptions if you choose a paid plan.
  • Improve reliability, security, and product quality.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the App (hosting, email delivery, payment processing) under contractual confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your account and profile data while your account is active. You may export or delete your data from Account settings in the App, or contact us to request deletion.

Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.
